Ten pouches for DS Satva — front and back

All ten carry Garam Masala so you are comparing the design, not the product. The back is identical on every one — it holds the matter the law requires, and that is not a style decision.

Shown at 64%. Ctrl+P prints them at true 100 × 150 mm, one pair per landscape page. Anything in red is a real gap I could not fill.
