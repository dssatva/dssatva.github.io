Twenty simple pouches — front and back

Quiet by construction: one ground, one ink, one accent used once, and a name big enough to read across a shop. All twenty carry Garam Masala so you are comparing design, not product.

Shown at 62%. Ctrl+P prints at true 100 × 150 mm. The logo is a third setting — muted like your tote, but dark enough to actually read (the soft one measured 1.5:1 on cream, which is why it looked washed out). Anything red is a real gap I still need from you.
