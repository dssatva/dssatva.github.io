Our story

Started in a kitchen,
not a boardroom

SATVA began with a simple, annoying discovery: the masala on the shelf did not taste like the masala at home. Not because the recipe was lost — because somewhere between the field and the pouch, things get added that were never spices.

So we started grinding small quantities ourselves, in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, and giving them to the people whose opinion is hardest to fool: homemakers and women running tiffin kitchens. Every batch since has been shaped by what they told us to fix.

Read how we test

Tested, not assumed

Every batch is judged
by the people who cook

We hand out free samples and then ask for the one thing most brands never want: what is wrong with it. Too spicy, not enough salt, colour off, smell weak — it all goes on a form, and the next batch changes because of it.

If you have a SATVA sample in your kitchen, please tell us honestly how it went. It takes two minutes.
