    Our story

    Rooted in experience.Driven by purity.

    Eighteen years watching the food supply chain showed our founder how far everyday staples have drifted from nutrition — synthetic aromas, anti-caking agents, chemical polishes. Satva (सत्त्व) is the essence of life: purity, truth and balance. We exist to bring it back to your daily kitchen.

    At DS Satva Foods, our story begins with a simple, unsettling realisation. Having spent over 18 years observing the modern food supply chain, our founder saw first-hand how the basic food on our plates has drifted away from true nutrition. Everyday kitchen staples — the very spices, pulses and grains meant to nourish our families — are increasingly loaded with synthetic aromas, anti-caking agents, chemical polishes and artificial preservatives.

    The result? Declining immunity, rising lifestyle disorders, and everyday meals that lack true nourishment.

    56.4% of India’s total disease burden is attributable to unhealthy diets.ICMR–NIN, Dietary Guidelines for Indians, May 2024

    We created DS Satva Foods to change that story.

    The word Satva (सत्त्व) is the absolute essence of life — purity, truth and balance. We exist to bring that exact purity back to your daily kitchen essentials. From naturally stone-ground spices that keep their essential oils, to unpolished pulses, nutrient-dense seeds and unrefined Pink Himalayan salt, every product we pack is clean, authentic and untouched by harmful chemicals.

    We believe health is not built in a pharmacy — it is built in your kitchen. When your daily ingredients are honest, your body thrives.
