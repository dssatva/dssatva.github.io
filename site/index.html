    Our story

    Rooted in experience.
    Driven by purity.

    At DS Satva Foods, our story begins with a simple, unsettling realisation. Having spent over 18 years observing the modern food supply chain, our founder saw first-hand how the basic food on our plates has drifted away from true nutrition. Everyday kitchen staples — the very spices, pulses and grains meant to nourish our families — are increasingly loaded with synthetic aromas, anti-caking agents, chemical polishes and artificial preservatives.

    The result? Declining immunity, rising lifestyle disorders, and everyday meals that lack true nourishment.

    56.4% of India’s total disease burden is attributable to unhealthy diets.ICMR–NIN, Dietary Guidelines for Indians, May 2024

    We created DS Satva Foods to change that story.

    The word Satva (सत्त्व) is the absolute essence of life — purity, truth and balance. We exist to bring that exact purity back to your daily kitchen essentials. From naturally stone-ground spices that keep their essential oils, to unpolished pulses, nutrient-dense seeds and unrefined Pink Himalayan salt, every product we pack is clean, authentic and untouched by harmful chemicals.

    We believe health is not built in a pharmacy — it is built in your kitchen. When your daily ingredients are honest, your body thrives.

    Our promise

    • 100% authentic & natural. No synthetic colours, artificial aromas or chemical preservatives.
    • Nutrient-preserved processing. Cold, naturally ground spices that keep their essential oils and aroma.
    • Unpolished goodness. Pulses and seeds packed in their raw, natural form — no dangerous polish, no moisture treatment.
    • Uncompromising standards. Sourced directly from trusted farms, tested for safety, packed with hygiene at the core.

    The paradox

    We study a phone for days.
    We buy our food out of habit.

    Buying a smartphone, clothes, or even a simple piece of furniture, most people spend days evaluating specifications, comparing features and reading reviews end to end, all to make an informed choice. Yet when it comes to the very food that enters our bodies every single day, that basic study is missing.

    In most Indian households daily groceries are bought out of routine — a familiar brand, a familiar price, simple convenience — and almost nobody stops to check how those staples were processed, or how far they have been processed down.

    Specifications compared. Features weighed. Reviews read to the last one.

    A phone · bought once in three years

    Fabric felt between the fingers. Finish inspected. Three shops before deciding.

    Clothes, furniture · bought once a season

    Reached for out of routine. Same packet, same shelf, into the bag.

    Today’s haldi and tikhat · eaten every single day
